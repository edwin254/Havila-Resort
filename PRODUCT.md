# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary decision-maker for this build: the owner of Havila Resort, who must see the site on their phone and want to buy it.

Site visitors:
- Nairobi residents planning a weekend or day trip (about two hours by road, mostly on mobile).
- HR and office admins organising team building, conferences and staff retreats, who need rates and capacity to get internal approval.
- Couples and families planning weddings and celebrations.
- Campers, adventure groups and non-resident travellers.

## Product Purpose
The resort's public website and direct-enquiry channel. Success means guests understand the place and send a complete, ready-to-answer enquiry directly rather than through a third party.

## Positioning
A resort on the River Tana with its own waterfall (Kirengete Falls), two islands, a hilltop restaurant facing Mt. Kenya and the Aberdares, and rafting, quad biking and camping on the grounds. Few neighbouring venues can truthfully combine all of those in one property.

## Operating Context
Static site with no backend. Enquiries are composed in the browser and sent through WhatsApp or email. Delivered as a live preview link the owner opens on their phone. Kenyan mobile data means page weight matters.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Name is spelled **Havila** (never "Havilla").
- Rooms: Standard and Executive deluxe rooms, each individually themed (modern, exotic, African), facing Kirengete Falls and Gaturi Hills. Camping grounds.
- Dining: Barizi Restaurant, on a hilltop, local and international cuisine, some produce from the garden.
- Activities: rafting, mini rafting, kayaking, boat rides, fishing, quad biking, cycling, archery, hiking, skywalk, trekking, nature trail, swaying bridge, yoga, pottery, apiculture and aquaculture training, team building.
- Tagline: The Gift of Serenity. The name comes from the biblical Garden of Eden.
- Events: event grounds and conferencing for weddings, sporting and corporate events.
- Check-in after 15:00, check-out before 10:00. No pets.
- Rates come from a third-party operator's 2026 listing (African Spice Safaris), so they are unverified and must be confirmed by the owner.
- Undecided or unknown: conference capacities, pool (no evidence, do not claim one), acreage (the site uses the official ten acres, another source says 40). The official site's phone number, +254 715 255007, is used; WhatsApp on it is unconfirmed.

## Brand Commitments
Existing name and domain, havilaresort.com. The logo has not been supplied, so a wordmark stands in.

## Evidence on Hand
- Google rating about 4.3 from about 939 reviews (read October 2026, to be re-checked).
- Three guest quotes come from the resort's own website. No other verbatim reviews, and no original photography or logo files, are on hand. Do not fabricate testimonials or present stock imagery as the real resort.
- Contact details conflict: +254 709 255006 (Google), +254 715 255007 (Facebook), +254 719 255005 (booking listing); havilaresort@gmail.com (Facebook). The address is right off A2 Road, Rukanga, Riandira, Sagana.

## Product Principles
- Sell the place only Havila has: the falls, the islands, the hill.
- Every price is findable in two taps; a corporate planner can get approval from the site alone.
- An enquiry should arrive complete, so the front desk can answer in one reply.
- Never state what has not been verified; flag it for the owner instead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion respected, usable on low-end Android over slow connections. Be honest that the waterfall, swaying bridge and camping area are reported as hard to reach for guests with limited mobility.
